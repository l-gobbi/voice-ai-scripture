# 0004: Visual-Only Sensory Feedback Without Device Haptics

We decided to keep recitation feedback strictly visual (via dynamic animated waveform bars and color-coded diff badges) rather than triggering device vibration/haptic motors on pass or failure.

Haptic vibrations during spiritual recitation and quiet contemplation can feel intrusive or jarring. Maintaining visual-only feedback preserves a serene, contemplative atmosphere for scripture meditation while providing clear, instantaneous feedback through the animated waveform bars.
