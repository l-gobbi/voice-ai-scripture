# Product Definition: Voice AI Scripture Memory

## Vision & Audience
A contemplative, distraction-free voice-first mobile app designed for Christian believers to memorize, meditate on, and recite holy scripture with effortless fidelity.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Frictionless Memorization**: Progressive cloze scaffolding (Echo Mode -> Partial Masking -> Complete Cloze) and spaced repetition with SM-2 at launch, graded by an Error Budget. FSRS is a possible future scheduler, to be evaluated by replaying the Review Log (ADR 0017).
- **Voice-First Verification**: On-device native speech transcription with cloud Whisper fallback to practice completely hands-free while walking, driving, or in morning devotions.
- **Topical & Custom Curation**: Bundled topical decks in English & Portuguese (WEB, KJV, Almeida, ONBV) plus manual / clipboard import for copyrighted translations.
- **Viral Fellowship**: 1-tap deck sharing via deep link / clipboard to share memory sets with church small groups and friends.

## Ergonomic & Mobile Constraints
- Offline-first: Full offline functionality for recitation and bundled canon passages.
- Contemplative quiet time: Zero gaudy gamification, flashing ads, or abrasive alarms.
- Ergonomic reach: Large comfortable tap zones (min 44pt touch targets, 64pt hero mic trigger).
