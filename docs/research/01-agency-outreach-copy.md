## Executive Summary & Outreach Philosophy

Reaching B2B marketing and web design agency founders on LinkedIn requires navigating an environment defined by extreme outreach fatigue:
- Agency founders receive **8–15 inbound sales DMs per day**, virtually all pitching "AI SDRs", "Guaranteed 20 qualified appointments", or "White-label fulfillment".
- Their spam radar triggers instantly on generic pleasantries (*"Hope you're having a productive week!"*), sycophantic flattery (*"Love what you're doing at [Agency]!"*), or unsolicited pitch slaps.
- They are time-starved and fiercely protective of their calendar. Any message asking for *"15 minutes to pick your brain"* or *"hop on a quick discovery call"* is immediately ignored or archived.

### The "Anti-Pitch" Philosophy
To achieve response rates above 25% from agency owners, the outreach must flip the typical sales dynamic:
1. **Peer-to-Peer, Operator-to-Operator Tone**: Written like a fellow agency operator sharing an experiment, not a junior SDR hunting for quota.
2. **Found Money Hypothesis**: Agency owners have already spent cash acquiring leads that stalled 3–12 months ago after a discovery call or proposal. They intuitively know that pipeline represents unharvested revenue, but they hate manual follow-ups.
3. **Frictionless Handoff**: No Calendly links, no sales calls, no onboarding forms, no software sign-up required.
4. **Radical Transparency**: Stating the exact "catch" upfront removes skepticism and builds immediate trust.
5. **Total Sending Sovereignty**: The agency founder retains 100% control over whether, when, and how messages are sent. Nothing touches their clients without their direct review.

## 1. Three Battle-Tested LinkedIn Cold DM Frameworks

All templates are designed to stay strictly under 70 words, use clean conversational syntax, and eliminate corporate buzzwords.

---

### Framework 1: The "Pipeline Graveyard" Observation (Direct & Relatable)

**Angle**: Leverages the universal agency reality that 60–80% of proposals ghost due to timing/budget rather than rejection.

#### Step 1: Connection Request Note (Max 300 characters / 38 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}} — noticed your work with {{niche/clientType}} at {{company}}. Quick question: do you guys have a pile of qualified proposals from last year that just went cold and never closed? Working on an experiment around this.*

#### Step 2: Main DM (First touch upon connection accepted — 56 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}}, thanks for connecting.*
> 
> *Most agency owners I talk to have 20–50 qualified leads from the past 12 months that ghosted after a proposal.*
> 
> *I built a lightweight system that writes tailored reactivation angles for dead leads. Mind if I write revival drafts for 5–10 of your cold leads for free?*
> 
> *Zero pitch, no call needed.*

#### Step 3: Follow-Up Nudge (4 days later — 24 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}} — totally fine if timing's bad. Just curious if reviving 1 or 2 old proposals is worth testing, or if pipeline's already full?*

---

### Framework 2: The "Found Money" Permission Hook (Value-First / Dean Jackson Adapted)

**Angle**: Treats dormant leads as liquid assets sitting in cold storage, offering bespoke work before asking for anything.

#### Step 1: Connection Request Note (Max 300 characters / 31 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}}, running a test on dormant pipeline revival specifically for {{agencyNiche}} agencies. Would love to connect and share what's working if you're open to it.*

#### Step 2: Main DM (First touch upon connection accepted — 48 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}} — appreciate the connect.*
> 
> *Quick question: if you gave me 10 leads that ghosted your team after a proposal or discovery call, could I write custom reactivation messages for them at zero cost?*
> 
> *If one books a call, you win a deal. If not, no harm done.*
> 
> *Worth a look?*

#### Step 3: Follow-Up Nudge (4 days later — 27 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}} — no worries if you're slammed. Just thought I'd check before I offer the audit slots to a couple of other {{agencyNiche}} founders this week.*

---

### Framework 3: The "Anti-SaaS Concierge" Pilot (Radical Honesty & Peer Angle)

**Angle**: Positions the outreach as an operator seeking real-world case studies for a new workflow tool rather than a vendor pitching a service.

#### Step 1: Connection Request Note (Max 300 characters / 35 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}} — building a workflow tool to reactivate ghosted agency leads. Not selling anything; looking for 3 founders in {{niche}} to run 10 cold contacts through it for feedback. Great to connect.*

#### Step 2: Main DM (First touch upon connection accepted — 62 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}}, thanks for connecting.*
> 
> *I’m stress-testing a new reactivation engine built specifically for marketing/web shops. Instead of asking you to buy another tool, I'd rather do the manual work for you.*
> 
> *If you share 10 stalled deals, I'll generate personalized revival drafts for each.*
> 
> *Takes 2 minutes on your end, no software to learn, no call. Open to testing it?*

#### Step 3: Follow-Up Nudge (5 days later — 21 words)
> *Hey {{firstName}} — just bumping this once in case it slipped. If you'd rather not disturb old contacts, no problem at all.*

## 2. The 2-Minute Low-Friction Intake Protocol

When an agency founder replies *"Sure, let's try it"* or *"How does this work?"*, **over 70% drop off if you send a Calendly link or an onboarding form.**

### The Immediate Response Script (Send immediately upon "Yes"):
> *Awesome. Super simple — you don't need to fill out any forms or connect your CRM.*
> 
> *Just pick whichever method is easiest for you:*
> 
> **Option A (Fastest — paste right here in DM):**  
> *Paste 3 to 10 bullet points with:*  
> *1. Prospect first name & company*  
> *2. What project/service you discussed*  
> *3. Why they stalled or last notes (e.g. "budget paused in Nov", "went with internal team", "ghosted after quote")*  
> 
> **Option B (Spreadsheet):**  
> *Drop them into a simple 3-column sheet (Name, Company, Past Notes).*  
> 
> **Option C (Email forward):**  
> *Forward 3–5 old ghosted email threads directly.*  
> 
> *I'll send back tailored reactivation drafts within 24 hours. You review everything before sending anything.*

## 3. Typical Objections & Handling Scripts

- **Objection 1: "I can't share client or prospect data / NDA concerns."**  
  *Script*: *"Completely understand. You don't need to share emails or real names. You can anonymize everything: e.g. 'B2B SaaS company, 40 employees, ghosted on a $10k website quote due to budget'. The engine only needs context to write the angle. You plug in real names when sending."*

- **Objection 2: "We already follow up regularly."**  
  *Script*: *"Makes total sense. Where this works best isn't on active leads, but on the ones marked 'Closed - Lost' or leads that went dark 6–18 months ago where your reps have completely stopped reaching out. Happy to test it on 5 leads your team wrote off. If nothing happens, zero lost time."*

- **Objection 3: "What's the catch? Why do this for free?"**  
  *Script*: *"Totally transparent: I'm stress-testing a new reactivation engine built for agencies. If 1 or 2 of these leads re-engage and you win a client, you might want to use the system for the rest of your dead CRM contacts ($99/mo). If not, you got 10 tailored emails for free, and I get feedback on how to improve the copy engine. Fair enough?"*

## 4. Delivery & Retainer Conversion Playbook

1. **Delivery (Within 24h)**:
   Deliver the 10 messages via a private Google Doc / Notion table, paired with a 60-second Loom explaining why each hook was chosen.
2. **Check-in (Day 4)**:
   *"Hey {{firstName}} — did you get a chance to fire off those 10 revival messages? Curious if anyone bit yet."*
3. **Conversion Close (Day 7)**:
   *"Hey {{firstName}} — checking in on the test. If you saw replies or booked a call: we can run your entire dormant CRM pipeline (up to 250 leads) through the engine for $99/mo, or I can run them concierge for you. Want me to set that up for the rest of your list?"*
