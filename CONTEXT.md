# CONTEXT.md - Ubiquitous Language & Domain Model

This document defines the shared vocabulary for this workspace. Use these terms precisely when formulating ideas, tickets, questions, and specifications.

## Core Concepts

- **Business Idea**: A proposed hypothesis describing a target audience, a painful unaddressed problem, a proposed solution, and a monetization mechanic.
- **Value Proposition**: The specific, quantifiable reason a target persona chooses this solution over existing alternatives or inertia.
- **Target Persona (ICP)**: The ideal customer profile who experiences the problem most acutely and holds purchasing authority or willingness to pay.
- **Tracer Bullet**: A minimal end-to-end slice through all layers (e.g. landing page + waitlist + interview invite) designed to test critical assumptions immediately with minimal waste.
- **Grilling Session**: A structured interview loop that systematically questions assumptions, explores edge cases, and discovers hidden constraints before building.
- **ADR (Architectural Decision Record)**: A short record documenting a key technical, business, or operational choice, its context, alternatives evaluated, and rationale.
