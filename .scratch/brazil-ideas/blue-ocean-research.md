# Brazilian B2B "Blue Ocean" Opportunities: The Unsexy & Uncrowded

This report identifies 5 highly specific, hyper-local, and "unsexy" B2B niches in Brazil. These sectors have been heavily filtered to exclude areas with obvious VC-backed incumbents (like generic PropTechs, standard HRTechs, or pure Fintechs). Instead, we focus on raw operational bottlenecks, regulatory quirks unique to Brazil, and highly fragmented informal markets.

---

## 1. Gestão e Conformidade para "Death Techs" (Cemitérios e Funerárias)

- **The Concept:** An all-in-one ERP/SaaS focused on the digitization of funeral homes and cemeteries. It manages recurring funeral plan contracts (planos funerários), CRM, physical grave space mapping, digital memorials, and tracks late payments.
- **The Target Niche:** Private cemetery administrators and local/regional funeral homes.
- **Why competition is currently low:** The "deathcare" sector is widely considered taboo and highly unsexy. VC funds historically avoid it due to lack of standard tech appeal, leaving the space to a few bootstrapped software providers (like Unymos or Heternum) despite being a massive R$ 13 billion/year market in Brazil.
- **Why the pain point is so high:** Selling pre-paid "planos funerários" relies heavily on recurring payments. Managing this via paper or generic ERPs leads to massive default rates (inadimplência). Furthermore, space management in cemeteries is often still done via analog maps, causing critical operational errors.

## 2. Conciliador PIX "Na Ponta" para Motoristas de Distribuidoras

- **The Concept:** A lightweight, mobile-first PIX conciliation app strictly for delivery drivers (truck drivers for beverage distributors, gas, water, wholesale food). It instantly verifies that a PIX payment sent by a small merchant (padaria, boteco) has landed in the distributor's bank account, without granting the driver access to the company's bank app.
- **The Target Niche:** Mid-to-large regional B2B distributors (e.g., Ambev partners, local wholesale food distributors).
- **Why competition is currently low:** Existing PIX conciliation software (Dattos, Bling) solves the problem for the back-office and e-commerce. No one is solving the "street-level" UX for the driver who is waiting at the bar's door.
- **Why the pain point is so high:** Delivery drivers lose 15-30 minutes per stop calling the finance department via WhatsApp to confirm if a PIX cleared. Alternatively, they accept fake PIX receipts, generating massive losses. Fixing this increases daily delivery volume per truck significantly.

## 3. Gestão e Logística para "Disk Entulho" e Caçambas

- **The Concept:** A vertical SaaS to manage the entire lifecycle of construction waste dumpsters (caçambas). It tracks dumpster locations via GPS/app, optimizes truck routing, manages billing, and automates the mandatory issuance of environmental documents like the MTR (Manifesto de Transporte de Resíduos).
- **The Target Niche:** Small to medium waste management and "Disk Entulho" companies heavily spread across Brazilian municipalities.
- **Why competition is currently low:** It is a deeply fragmented, highly informal market. There are a few specialized tools (e.g., Caçambas Online, Bascully), but zero VC-backed "unicorns" sucking the air out of the room. It’s too operational and dirty for traditional tech investors.
- **Why the pain point is so high:** A forgotten dumpster on a public street generates massive city fines. Discarding waste without a proper MTR can lead to severe environmental penalties or closure. Margins are tight, so optimized routing saves thousands of Reais in diesel fuel per month.

## 4. Gestão de Escalas e GESP para Segurança Privada

- **The Concept:** A regulatory and operational SaaS for private security companies. It handles complex Brazilian labor shifts (e.g., 12x36), geolocation-based digital point clocking for remote guards, and automates compliance integration with the Federal Police's mandatory system (GESP - Gestão Eletrônica de Segurança Privada).
- **The Target Niche:** Third-party armed and unarmed security guard providers (vigilância patrimonial).
- **Why competition is currently low:** The sector is heavily unionized, hyper-regulated by the Federal Police, and deals with firearms. Many ESG-driven VC funds have mandates explicitly preventing them from investing in the private security or firearms ecosystem, keeping competition artificially low.
- **Why the pain point is so high:** If a Federal Police inspector finds a guard at a post with an expired license (reciclagem) or carrying an unregistered firearm, the company faces crippling fines or loss of operating license. Furthermore, miscalculating the 12x36 shift scale generates massive labor lawsuits (passivos trabalhistas).

## 5. Sistema "All-In-One" para Associações de Proteção Veicular (APV)

- **The Concept:** A complete operational system for APVs. It handles recurring billing, integration with the FIPE table for dynamic pricing, mobile damage inspections (vistoria prévia), and automated calculation of monthly damage apportionment (rateio) among members.
- **The Target Niche:** APVs (Mutual vehicle protection associations), which act as informal insurance providers, primarily dominant in Brazil's Northeast and the peripheries of large cities.
- **Why competition is currently low:** APVs operate in a legal gray area—they are legal as "cooperatives" but are not regulated by SUSEP like standard insurers. Because of this regulatory ambiguity, VC funds and large tech companies avoid them entirely.
- **Why the pain point is so high:** APVs have enormous cash flow but suffer heavily from fraudulent claims, fake inspections, and the mathematical complexity of dividing the cost of stolen/crashed cars among thousands of members at the end of every month. They are desperate for robust tech to professionalize their operations.
