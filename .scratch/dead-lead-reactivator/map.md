# Wayfinder Map: Dead Lead Reactivator

Status: active
Effort: dead-lead-reactivator

## Destination

Acquire the first 3 paying B2B marketing agency customers for Dead Lead Reactivator at \$99+/mo, powered by a functional V1 concierge prototype and a proven direct pilot acquisition loop.

## Notes

- **Domain**: B2B Digital Marketing & Web Agencies (contract sizes \$2k–\$10k).
- **Execution Model**: Concierge MVP first. Manually run free 10-lead audits for agency founders using our local engine, deliver high-converting drafts, and close them on \$99/mo to process their full pipeline.
- **Skills to consult**: `/grilling`, `/domain-modeling`, `/prototype`, `/research`.

## Decisions so far

- [01: Research High-Converting LinkedIn Outreach Copy for Agency Owners](docs/research/01-agency-outreach-copy.md): Established 3 battle-tested cold DM frameworks (Pipeline Graveyard, Found Money, Anti-SaaS Concierge), the 2-minute in-chat intake protocol, objection scripts, and 7-day conversion cadence.

## Not yet specified

- **Payment Collection & Billing**: Whether to use a simple Stripe Payment Link, invoice, or checkout page for the \$99/mo subscription.
- **SaaS Web App Migration Threshold**: The exact revenue or volume trigger at which we graduate from concierge local execution to a self-serve Next.js web application.
- **Win-Rate Tracking**: Lightweight mechanism to follow up with agency founders after 7 days to see which reactivated leads booked discovery calls.

## Out of Scope

- Automated bidirectional CRM integrations (HubSpot/Salesforce OAuth).
- Custom multi-tenant authentication, user management, and organization roles prior to 3 paying clients.
- Automated email sending / SMTP sequencing within the app.
- Phone/Voice AI agents or WhatsApp/SMS spam.
