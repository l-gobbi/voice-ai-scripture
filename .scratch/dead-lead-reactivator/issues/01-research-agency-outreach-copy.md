# 01: Research High-Converting LinkedIn Outreach Copy for Agency Owners

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: None

## Question

What is the most effective, zero-sales-pressure LinkedIn cold DM strategy and message structure to propose a "Free 10-Lead Revival Audit" to B2B digital marketing and web design agency founders?

Specifically investigate:
1. Low-friction conversational openers that get replies from agency founders without sounding like typical "lead gen agency" spam.
2. The specific questions to ask to collect the 10 cold leads and agency context quickly without overwhelming them.
3. Proven copy examples and psychological hooks that frame this as a zero-risk, high-upside test.

## Answer

Comprehensive research completed and captured at [`docs/research/01-agency-outreach-copy.md`](file:///c:/Users/Lucas%20Gobbi/business-ideas/docs/research/01-agency-outreach-copy.md).

Key resolutions:
1. **Three battle-tested copy frameworks**: "Pipeline Graveyard", "Found Money Hook", and "Anti-SaaS Concierge" (<65 words, zero pitch, no calls requested).
2. **2-Minute In-Chat Intake Protocol**: Bypasses Calendly/onboarding forms by allowing founders to paste 3–10 bullet points directly into LinkedIn chat or an anonymized spreadsheet.
3. **Disarming Objections**: Handled data privacy (anonymization), "we already follow up" (closed-lost focus), and "what's the catch" (radical transparency on the $99/mo full list conversion).
4. **Delivery & 7-Day Conversion Sequence**: Prescribes delivery format (Doc + 60s Loom) and Day 4/Day 7 check-in scripts to close the \$99/mo subscription.
